# 1.6: quality recovery and the supply guarantee

Status: implemented (code) — pending migration 007 + live recovery run. Target: Oct 28-30. Effort: 12-16 focused hours. Depends on: 1.2, 1.4, 1.5. Next: [1.7](1.7-buyer-and-hub-product-experience.md).

**Outcome:** the platform actively resolves a paid order's supply failure. This is the main differentiating demonstration: an inspection reduces accepted coverage, AI selects a valid replacement, and FarmBroker restores the original commitment or follows its refund policy.

## Implementation sequence

1. Make inspections append-only evidence records. Calculate accepted coverage independently from allocated quantity and record per-lot responsibility transfer.
2. Define the minimum guarantee policy: deliver the accepted contracted quantity/specification/deadline, or resolve nonfulfillment with a full refund. Do not silently reduce quantity or raise the buyer charge.
3. When inspection fails or partially accepts a lot, create a unique shortage incident and a recovery run. Generate feasible replacement candidates using current uncommitted supply.
4. Let AI select a valid replacement or policy remedy. Validate against original quote, remaining time, cost/exposure cap, and inspection rules; commit through the 1.4 reservation service.
5. After replacement inspection and simulated handover confirmation, finalize supplier entitlements and fulfillment. If recovery is impossible, invoke the 1.5 refund service and retain platform liabilities.

## File, data, and API targets

Extend `lib/agents/quality.ts`, `agents/finance.ts`, `api/quality`, `api/settle`, procurement/run tools, and Hub inspection pages. New suggested modules: `src/lib/recovery/policy.ts`, `service.ts`, `src/lib/quality/coverage.ts`.

Suggested migration `007_quality_recovery.sql`: append-only `inspections`, `shortage_incidents`, `recovery_attempts`, `responsibility_events`, and simulated handover evidence. Reference allocation attempt, actor, measurement snapshot, policy version, and operation ID. Correction events supersede evidence; they do not erase it.

## Implementation checklist

- [ ] Persist measured received weight, usable quantity, condition, quality inputs, decision, inspector, and timestamp per allocation attempt.
- [ ] Allow zero received weight to record a no-show. Reject negative, nonfinite, or impossible usable quantities.
- [ ] Bound accepted quantity by received and allocated quantities. Keep excess stock outside this deal unless explicitly contracted.
- [ ] Version simulated thresholds and identify product form/specification. Current moisture/visual rules are demonstration rules, not agronomic certification.
- [ ] Distinguish allocated coverage, inspected accepted coverage, pending inspection, rejected quantity, and remaining target.
- [ ] Count partial lots by usable quantity. A passed classification alone cannot hide a weight shortage.
- [ ] Transfer simulated responsibility only for accepted quantity; rejected quantity remains supplier responsibility under the demo policy.
- [ ] Calculate supplier entitlement for accepted usable kg at its contractual rate, consistently for passed and partial lots.
- [ ] Keep accepted supplier payables even if FarmBroker later refunds the buyer; platform responsibility has financial consequences.
- [ ] Create one shortage incident per triggering inspection/correction; repeated webhook/browser requests do not create repeated recovery loops.
- [ ] Pause settlement and completion while accepted quantity is below target or a recovery/refund is unresolved.
- [ ] Provide AI with grounded replacement candidates, cost impact, available time, and policy limits. Persist selection and validation evidence.
- [ ] Execute replacement reservations only after payment, using current stock and an operation key tied to incident/attempt.
- [ ] Release/quarantine rejected supply according to policy. Do not reselect the same rejected lot to erase the problem.
- [ ] Stay inside the original buyer terms and modeled platform exposure cap. No automatic new buyer charge or unauthorized scope change.
- [ ] Require the replacement's own Hub inspection. Choosing or reserving a backup does not count as fulfillment.
- [ ] Set bounded recovery attempts/deadline; exhausting them transitions to refund eligibility or explicit needs-review, not an infinite loop.
- [ ] Invoke the real sandbox full-refund service only under the documented guarantee policy. Show requested/pending state until provider completion.
- [ ] Keep refunded and fulfilled outcomes distinct. A manual review path has an assigned next action and cannot appear as success.
- [ ] Require a labeled simulated handover/receipt event before fulfillment verification; do not imply a carrier integration exists.
- [ ] Finalize settlement calculation once, after accepted coverage and handover gates. Supplier payout UI says “Calculated; simulated payout”, not “Sent”.
- [ ] Record modeled platform reserve/cost consumption, remaining exposure, and original gross spread separately from realized profit.
- [ ] Provide three isolated, reproducible scenario fixtures: all-pass, 2,000 kg rejected with backup, and no feasible backup/refund.
- [ ] Make scenario injection a Hub-only demo operation with explicit simulated evidence and unique IDs; never edit payment state to start the story.
- [ ] Update the guarantee explanation, quality/finance documentation, and user stories.

## Acceptance and verification

1. Happy path: 10,000 kg accepted, handover simulated, settlement calculated, persisted fulfilled outcome. Locked legacy money still matches before fees/costs.
2. Recovery path: reject the 2,000 kg lot. Accepted coverage becomes 8,000/10,000, completion is blocked, and AI selects a feasible standby lot. After its reservation **and inspection**, coverage returns to 10,000 and fulfillment becomes eligible.
3. Partial path: only part of a lot is usable. Entitlement and shortage use actual accepted kg; a replacement covers exactly the gap without over-allocation.
4. Refund path: no affordable/on-time backup exists. No false fulfilled banner appears. One sandbox refund is requested, reconciled, and displayed with its provider ID.
5. Exposure path: some lots were already accepted before failure. Their supplier payables remain visible after the buyer refund; the finance view shows platform exposure rather than extra margin.
6. Replay the failure event and recovery advance operation concurrently. Exactly one incident/attempt reservation commits and no duplicate refund begins.
7. Turn off the model during recovery. The order remains recoverable/paused with its audit history; it cannot claim autonomous replacement from a canned message.
8. Refresh midway through replacement inspection or refund pending. Persisted coverage, responsibilities, and remedy status survive.

## Scope cut and exit gate

Minimum: one failed-lot recovery, actual accepted-quantity accounting, one full-refund fallback, and explicit responsibility/entitlement evidence. Cut partial buyer delivery, multi-incident optimization, automatic reorders, real insurance, carrier APIs, and supplier messaging.

**Exit gate:** the flagship failure/recovery story works with actual AI and PayPal sandbox evidence, and a no-backup run resolves honestly without fulfilled/profit mislabeling.
