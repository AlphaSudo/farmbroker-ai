# 2.6 — Incremental repair and bounded platform exposure

**Status: implemented (code) — live recovery + exposure proof pending paid flow.**

**Outcome:** recover only the shortage, preserve accepted stock, and keep the buyer's charge fixed. Effort: 12 hours. Depends on 2.2 decisions, 2.4 durable execution, and 2.5 planning.

## Implementation sequence

1. Define a versioned recovery policy: trigger after all current inspections finish, accepted kg target, maximum attempts/deadline, eligible replacement spec, supplier rate floor, maximum additional entitlement, and explicit review/refund conditions.
2. Snapshot entitlement terms per allocation. New v2 orders can pay a replacement's accepted kg under its stored rate rather than assume every future lot fits the original uniform clearing rate. Preserve v1 behavior/history and the baseline fixture's 75,000 EGP entitlement.
3. Compute remaining authority from accepted supplier obligations, provider fees/reserves where known, and the configured platform budget. A replacement may consume margin within that limit; no new buyer charge or unsupported capital is assumed. Label any additional platform-risk capital as simulated.
4. Build the repair subproblem from the missing kg and affected evidence only. Accepted lots/obligations are immutable constraints. Exclude quarantined/rejected lots, account for current commitments across orders, and select a valid stored replacement plan.
5. Enqueue recovery from the inspection/shortage event when eligible. Within a previously accepted policy, AI selection and validated commitment advance without a new operator button. Material buyer-term changes and authority overruns require explicit review.
6. Atomically commit the replacement decision, allocation/rate snapshot, incident/attempt, spend reservation, event, summary, and next job. Inspect replacement normally; only accepted kg closes the incident.
7. If no permitted repair exists, preserve accepted obligations and request the policy-authorized full refund through the durable PayPal flow. Expose pending/review/refunded truth and platform exposure separately from fulfillment/profit.

## Detailed checklist

- [ ] Policy version and prior authority bind the accepted quote.
- [ ] Each allocation stores immutable entitlement rate/currency/terms.
- [ ] Historical v1 orders are not retroactively repriced.
- [ ] Replacement supplier floor is honored by its actual entitlement.
- [ ] Buyer price/currency/quantity/specification stay unchanged.
- [ ] Accepted 8T and its obligations remain fixed when 2T fails.
- [ ] Rejected stock is quarantined and never reselected.
- [ ] Repair uses current global free stock, not only the current deal's allocations.
- [ ] Remaining-spend calculation includes already owed accepted goods.
- [ ] Unknown fees/risk-capital assumptions are visible and conservative.
- [ ] Extra recovery authority cannot be invented by the model.
- [ ] Selection + reservation + spend + incident + job commit together.
- [ ] Duplicate inspection/events/jobs do not open duplicate incidents or spend twice.
- [ ] Recovery waits for required inspections and runs independently of a browser.
- [ ] Repeated/conflicting/stale repair attempts have bounded outcomes.
- [ ] Replacement must be inspected before shortage resolves.
- [ ] No backup, exhausted attempts, deadline, or budget failure reaches refund/review.
- [ ] Confirmed full refund does not erase accepted supplier payables/platform inventory.
- [ ] Pending/failed refund cannot display Refunded or Fulfilled.
- [ ] Scenario cost/exposure evidence distinguishes modeled valuation from actual provider money.
- [ ] Baseline 78,000 / 75,000 / 3,000 math remains unchanged.
- [ ] Compare minimal repair against full rebuild on the same disruptions.

## Acceptance scenarios and targets

A 10T order rejects 2T and accepts 8T. Automatically select and commit a pre-existing permitted replacement, inspect it, and reach exactly 10T without modifying the original charge or accepted items.

Use a second versioned fixture with a more expensive replacement inside the margin budget: entitlements increase transparently and gross spread falls. A third fixture exceeds authority/no backup: no unauthorized commitment occurs; actual full refund/review follows, accepted payables remain visible.

Internal recovery target ≤2 s excluding provider/model/queue time; report changed items, retained accepted kg, actual full completion time, and budget utilization. This target does not promise physical delivery speed.

## Rollout and cut

New policies apply to new v2 fixtures only. Shadow exposure math against recorded obligations before automatic execution. Stop admission if obligation reconciliation drifts; keep existing payment/refund reconciliation available.

Cut probabilistic failure models and complex platform-capital simulations. Keep exact kg, stored rates, spend cap, immutable accepted stock, and a real refund path. **Judge proof:** the system automatically restores a paid promise inside prior authority and explains the financial consequence.
