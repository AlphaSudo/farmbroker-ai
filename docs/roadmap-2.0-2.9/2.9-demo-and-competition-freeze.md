# 2.9 — Release freeze, working video, and judge package

**Status: packaging ready — video/submit actions await user authorization.**

**Outcome:** judges can understand and verify the strongest implementation within the submission format. Effort: 12 hours. Depends on evidence gates; rehearsals begin during 2.3.

## Implementation sequence

1. Select the final release commit/migrations/configuration and freeze feature work. Resolve release-blocking bugs, rerun affected checks, and update footage if behavior changes. Keep an explicit live/simulated/future capability table.
2. Prepare isolated happy, recovery, and refund scenarios with backup present before failure. Record actual PayPal/AI/run/plan/receipt IDs. Ensure the demonstrated AI selection is the selected plan that executed.
3. Film the functioning product with one continuous primary order and a labeled separate refund order. Capture actual browser approval/capture, genuine persisted recovery, and final handover/receipt; do not substitute provider mocks.
4. Use a concise architecture/performance overlay showing one verified before/after result and the native mechanism behind it. The product remains legible at normal video resolution.
5. Complete public repository/license/setup/tool disclosures, hosted demo/testing access, accessible screenshots, and a short honest audience/impact statement. Package current evidence instead of stale TODO capture IDs.
6. Have a new reviewer follow the judge guide without chat history; verify all public links, permissions, configuration instructions, and sandbox access. Provide scoped testing access rather than unrestricted service/operator keys.
7. Submit by the internal deadline, retain confirmation, and document who keeps the deployment/model quota/sandbox available through judging.
