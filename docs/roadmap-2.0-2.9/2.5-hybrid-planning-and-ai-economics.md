# 2.5 — Hybrid planning and economical intelligence

**Status: implemented (code) — live-model ablation sample pending.**

**Outcome:** useful AI interpretation/selection plus exact, bounded optimization. Effort: 16 hours. Depends on 2.1/2.2 snapshots/contracts and 2.4 workers.

## Implementation sequence

1. Expand confirmed intent to supported real purchasing constraints: crop, quantity, date, budget, grade/specification, and qualitative priorities such as fewer deliveries or origin diversity. Preserve original text/evidence references where consented. Unknown units/specifications require clarification.
2. Normalize semantics with AI into an allowlisted schema; code validates units/currency/bounds. Buyer confirmation creates the specification version before quote/payment. AI cannot infer unsupported weather, certification, or supplier facts.
3. Produce genuinely distinct feasible plans: cost-first, evidence-backed reliability, concentration/diversity, and low-disruption recovery where appropriate. Deduplicate identical items; expose measured metrics and evidence, not just names.
4. Use deterministic greedy/prefix plans plus a bounded improvement pass. Maintain a feasible non-dominated candidate set. An exact small-instance oracle evaluates the heuristic offline; do not call a heuristic globally optimal without proof.
5. Ask the model to select a known candidate according to confirmed priorities and supplied evidence. Validate ID/evidence/policy and compile it into the 2.2 executable decision. Distinguish preference satisfaction from physical feasibility.
6. Add content-addressed reuse/single-flight for identical scoped planning inputs. Keys cover spec, evidence/snapshot, policy, model, prompt, and relevant lot versions. Revalidate reused plans before execution; unrelated stock changes should not invalidate all work.
7. Add task/latency/token routing: small structured intent request, bounded plan selection, escalation for supported ambiguity. One overall deadline limits primary/fallback calls; rate-limited work waits visibly rather than fan-out to many models.

## Acceptance and performance

Held-out briefs include ambiguous bags, mixed units, missing budget, contradictory dates, and supported qualitative preferences. A changed confirmed priority selects a different valid plan on a fixture with a genuine tradeoff. Adversarial text cannot invent stock or authorize spending.

Compare cost-first, optimizer-only, and AI+optimizer under identical snapshots. Publish cases where AI adds latency without benefit. Healthy admitted planning target p95 ≤8 s; report live sample size, tokens/calls, queue wait, and failure rate. Do not claim free-model availability or intelligence improvement without evidence.

## Rollout and cut

Version new policies/prompts; retain old decision records. Shadow new candidate metrics against deterministic validation before admitting v2 orders. Preserve the baseline arithmetic fixture.

Cut exact large-scale optimization, extra models, broad language coverage, embeddings/RAG, and recursive agents. Keep one high-quality interpretation/selection path with bounded compute and measured contribution. **Judge proof:** AI understands a messy purchasing priority and selects a valid different action that actually executes.
